### The 20% "Needle Movers" that Drive 80% of Results

1.  **Contextual Continuity (The "Cocktail Party Effect"):** The human brain is hard-wired to ignore general information but focus intensely on anything related to its own identity or property. By using the exact Google Maps image of *Carrer de Valencia 214* on the landing page, you leverage "Pre-Suasion"—arranging for the audience to be sympathetic to your message before they even hear it.
2.  **The Opportunity Switch:** Russell Brunson argues that people do not want an "improvement offer" (saving money on a bill) because it requires them to admit their past decisions were wrong. Instead, you must offer a **New Opportunity** (Energy Independence) that replaces their current status as an "Energy Tenant" with that of an "Energy Owner".
3.  **The Value Equation (The "Diagnosis"):** Since you cannot provide real savings without a bill, your product is not "solar" yet—it is the **Audit**. Alex Hormozi’s Value Equation dictates that value increases when you decrease "Effort and Sacrifice". Framing the phone call as a "Technical Validation" rather than a sales pitch turns the salesperson into a "Doctor" providing a diagnosis, which has higher perceived status and lower resistance.
4.  **Micro-Commitment Momentum:** Using Robert Cialdini’s "Consistency Principle," you should never start with a phone number field. By getting the prospect to answer 1–2 simple questions first (an "Ask Campaign"), they become psychologically invested in finishing the process to remain consistent with their own actions.

#### 3. Hyper-Local Social Proof (The "Local Dominance Effect")
Cialdini’s "Similarity Principle" states that we follow the lead of "comparable others".
*   **Nudge Notification:** A small pop-up stating: *"Un propietario en Carrer de Valencia 212 acaba de recibir su diagnóstico"*.
*   **The "Neighbors" Map:** A small map snippet showing nearby blocks with pins labeled "Instalación Eltex Finalizada".

Do not lead with a "Contact Us" box.
*   **Step 1:** "¿Cuál es su prioridad: eliminar el coste de su factura o aumentar el valor patrimonial de su piso?" (Two-choice buttons).
*   **Step 2:** "¿Su gasto de luz mensual es superior o inferior a 100€?".
*   **Step 3 (Phone Capture):** Use the "Because" trigger.
