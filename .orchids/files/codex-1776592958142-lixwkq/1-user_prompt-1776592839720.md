To maximize conversion for the Carrer de Valencia 214 campaign, the landing page must transition from a physical anchor (the house image) to a digital "Diagnosis" Lead Magnet. Following the frameworks of Hormozi, Brunson, and Cialdini, here is the end-to-end structure.

### 1. The Pre-Suasive Hero Section (Contextual Continuity)
The primary objective here is to prevent a "Frankenstein experience" where the digital page feels disconnected from the postcard.

*   **Visual Background:** The hero image MUST be the same Google Maps capture of Carrer de Valencia 214 with the solar overlay used on the postcard. This utilizes "Contextual Prospecting," signaling to the buyer that the message is uniquely relevant to them.
*   **The "Opportunity Switch" Headline:** Use Russell Brunson’s formula: **How to [Result] without [Fear]**.
    *   *Draft:* "Carrer de Valencia 214: Cómo independizar su hogar de las subidas eléctricas sin cambiar su estilo de vida."
*   **The "Because" Justification:** Explicitly state *why* they are here. Provide a "Reason Why" to increase compliance.
    *   *Draft:* "Hacemos este mapeo porque estamos validando la eficiencia técnica de su manzana tras una instalación cercana."

### 2. The Lead Magnet: "The 2026 Solar Efficiency Roadmap"
Since you lack their actual bill, you are providing a **Diagnosis Lead Magnet**. According to Alex Hormozi, this is a "complete solution to a narrow problem" that reveals a larger problem solved by your core offer.

*   **Content:** Frame the free audit not as a "sales call," but as a high-value technical asset.
*   **The "Stack Slide":** Enumerate exactly what they receive to increase the "Price-to-Value Discrepancy".
    1.  **Mapeo de Incidencia Solar:** Precision satellite analysis of their roof's orientation.
    2.  **Shadow Analysis (Análisis de Sombras):** Identification of obstacles from nearby Eixample buildings.
    3.  **Local Subsidy Roadmap:** Specific grants available for their block this month.
    4.  **The "Hidden Cost" Report:** A breakdown of projected "rented energy" waste over the next 10 years if they do not act.

### 4. The Lead Capture: The "Ask Campaign" (Micro-Commitments)
Instead of a direct request for a phone number, use the "Ask Campaign" method to build consistency.

*   **Step 1: The Micro-commitment.** Ask a simple, qualitative question: *"¿Cuál es su prioridad principal: reducir su factura mensual o aumentar el valor patrimonial de su propiedad?"* [Discussion].

*   **Step 3: The Phone Capture (The "Hand-Hold").** After they make two micro-commitments, they are psychologically driven to remain consistent.
    *   *Copy:* "Nuestro sistema ha pre-calificado su tejado. Ingrese su teléfono para que un técnico valide los últimos 3 puntos de su Informe 2026. (Es una llamada de validación técnica, no comercial)."

Hormozi’s "Perfect Profit Combination" involves high demand and limited perceived supply.

*   **Urgency:** A countdown timer matching the postcard's deadline: *"Válida hasta el 13 de mayo"*.
